# Caravan-Inspired BJ Electronics Marketplace

Reference URL: `https://caravan.com.bd/`

The implementation uses the reference as structural inspiration for a Bangladesh-focused marketplace experience while retaining the original BJ Electronics brand, source catalog, transactional cart, checkout, inventory and administration system.

## Adapted marketplace patterns

- multi-level utility, search and category navigation;
- campaign-led hero area with secondary offer tiles;
- fast access to categories, deals, brands, tracking and customer care;
- live deal zone derived from real catalog comparison prices;
- popular brand/collection discovery derived from published product names;
- arrival, featured and high-availability product merchandising;
- confidence, delivery, warranty, returns and secure-checkout messaging;
- mobile-first navigation and responsive commerce layouts;
- order-tracking entry that preserves private access-token protection.

## Brand and content safeguards

- no Caravan logo, photography, copy or proprietary visual asset is copied;
- BJ Electronics blue, red, charcoal and white remain authoritative;
- products, prices, stock and offers remain sourced from the BJ Electronics database;
- no discount countdown or payment method is displayed unless backed by configured catalog or checkout functionality.
