# Caravan-inspired storefront implementation

This document records the implementation brief for a marketplace-style BJ Electronics storefront inspired by the information architecture and shopping density of the supplied Caravan reference, while preserving the BJ Electronics visual identity and original copy.

## Principles

- BJ Electronics blue/red/charcoal brand system remains authoritative.
- Do not copy third-party trademarks, product copy, imagery, or proprietary assets.
- Use the existing PostgreSQL catalog, cart, checkout, order, inventory, realtime and admin workflows.
- Keep the storefront responsive, accessible, indexable, and deployment-safe.

## Implemented surfaces

- marketplace utility bar and sticky commerce header;
- searchable catalog with category selector;
- expandable department menu and dense category navigation;
- three-column marketplace hero with department rail and promotional modules;
- trust and delivery strip;
- quick category discovery;
- deal, arrival and featured-product merchandising;
- featured-brand and lifestyle discovery modules;
- business-sales callout;
- richer customer-service and payment footer;
- mobile bottom navigation.
