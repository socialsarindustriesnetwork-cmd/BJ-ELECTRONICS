# Caravan Reference Storefront Rebuild

## Objective

Rebuild the BJ Electronics public store using the supplied `caravan.com.bd` reference as a structural and interaction benchmark while retaining original BJ Electronics branding, content, product data and artwork.

## Implemented experience

- announcement bar, marketplace header, department selector, search and shopping actions;
- desktop mega-department menu and mobile navigation;
- department rail, campaign hero and secondary promotional cards;
- nationwide-delivery, warranty, assistance and checkout trust strip;
- home-appliance and electronics category system;
- timed deals, featured catalog, leading brands, campaigns, lifestyle collections and new arrivals;
- product cards with discounts, stock, brand, category, wishlist and transactional cart actions;
- catalog filters for department, brand, price, availability and sorting;
- appliance-oriented product details with gallery, stock, buying information, purchase controls, description/specification area and recommendations;
- order tracking and complete customer-service, policy and company routes;
- complete marketplace footer with department navigation, customer service, payment and security information;
- responsive desktop, tablet and mobile layouts.

## Originality and safety

The implementation does not copy Caravan trademarks, logo files, product photos, written content or proprietary source code. It recreates the general information architecture and commerce interaction patterns using BJ Electronics assets and the repository's existing PostgreSQL-backed catalog, cart, checkout, inventory, order and realtime services.

## Verification

The repository audit validates the marketplace routes, taxonomy, navigation, responsive stylesheet, support pages and purchase flows. CI must additionally pass migration validation, environment validation, TypeScript, ESLint and production builds for both store and admin applications.
