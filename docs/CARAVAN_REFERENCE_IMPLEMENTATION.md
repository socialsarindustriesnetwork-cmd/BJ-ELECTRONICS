# Caravan-inspired BJ Electronics marketplace implementation

The public storefront adopts the reference site's dense category-led merchandising and information architecture while retaining BJ Electronics branding, original copy, original components and the existing production commerce systems.

## Implemented structure

- support-first utility bar, searchable header, cart value, wishlist and profile controls;
- department navigation, promotional hero and side campaigns;
- popular department discovery;
- Top Demand, TV & Entertainment, Home Appliances, Laptops & Computing and Audio & Headphones collections;
- View All and incremental Load More product controls;
- flash sale, featured products, new arrivals, campaign banners and brand discovery;
- newsletter, customer-feedback, service-assurance and information footer sections;
- About, Contact, Track Order, FAQ, Shipping & Returns, Warranty, Terms and Privacy pages;
- responsive desktop, tablet and mobile layouts.

## Production systems preserved

- PostgreSQL-backed product and inventory data;
- realtime catalog refresh;
- secure cart session and transactional cart updates;
- checkout validation and atomic inventory reservation;
- private order-confirmation and order-tracking access;
- canonical storefront and isolated administration origins;
- Hostinger release verification and fail-closed repository quality gates.

## Intellectual-property boundary

No Caravan branding, medical product content, contact details, product images, proprietary source code or copied page text is included. Automated audits prevent known reference-site identifiers from entering the BJ Electronics public source.

Canonical production domain: `https://www.bjelectronics.shop`.
