# Development Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation

- Responsive application shell
- Design tokens and reusable interface patterns
- Light and dark themes
- Executive overview dashboard
- Official brand asset architecture
- PWA metadata and CI checks

## Phase 2 — Security and access

- Authentication and session management
- Multi-factor authentication option
- Role-based access control
- Granular permission policies
- Immutable audit log

## Phase 3 — Catalog and inventory

- Products, variants, brands, categories and collections
- Product media and publication workflow
- Warehouses, stock levels, reservations, transfers and adjustments
- Supplier and purchase-order workflows

## Phase 4 — Commerce operations

- Orders, payments, fulfilment, shipping, returns and refunds
- Customer profiles, communication history and segmentation
- Coupons, promotions, campaigns and storefront content

## Phase 5 — Intelligence and integrations

- Sales, product, customer and inventory reports
- Payment, shipping, email and SMS adapters
- Background jobs, webhook delivery and retry management
- Permission-aware AI administrative assistant
